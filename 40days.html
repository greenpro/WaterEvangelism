Day 5

You will keep him in perfect peace,
Whose mind is stayed on You,
Because he trusts in You.
Isaiah 26:3


Pray for all volunteers and their families as they prepare for and participate in this ministry.
Day 4

The Lord shall preserve you from all evil; He shall preserve your soul.
The Lord shall preserve your going out and your coming in from this time forth, and even forevermore.
Psalm 121:7-8


Pray for the safety of all the volunteers and everyone involved in the ministry.
Day 3

And my God shall supply all your needs according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus.
Philippians 4:19


Pray for God to provide many volunteers to help with this ministry.
Day 2

A man’s heart plans his way,
But the Lord directs his steps.
Proverbs 16:9


Pray that the Holy Spirit will determine the locations for distributing the bottled water.
Day 1

Make a joyful shout to the Lord, all you lands!
Serve the Lord with gladness;
Come before His presence with singing.
Know that the Lord, He is God;
It is He who has made us, and not we ourselves;
We are His people and the sheep of His pasture.
Enter into His gates with thanksgiving,
And into His courts with praise.
Be thankful to Him, and bless His name.
For the Lord is good;
His mercy is everlasting,
And His truth endures to all generations.
  Psalm 100


Pray for all honor, glory, and praise be given to God in this ministry.
