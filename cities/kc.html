Go Share Jesus with all the World – Right here in Kansas City.

Join Us!

If you are looking to participate in Water Evangelism, We can help. Send us a message either through the contact in the lower right corner of the screen, by emailing info@waterevangelism.com, or by calling (913) 358-7872. We can work with you to get qr code stickers, and, if needed, water. You may distribute water throughout the area as a part of a single event or a longer campaign. See the tips and guidelines for further information.

Water
  • If your church chooses to raise funds for water, we have found Sam's Club the best place to buy. You can order water by the pallet or half pallet. The initial cost is $500 per pallet, though if ordering a lot you may be able to talk them down to $200 per.
  • When distributing water, we suggest using rolling coolers, or pull behind wagons to make it easier to haul.
  • Shopping centers and places where people are going between restaurants, are less ideal.
  • Distributing water in groups tends to not only be more fun, it also makes people less likely to take water.
QR Codes
  • The qr codes should be placed prominently on the bottle so it can be easily seen.
  • Make sure the label is not crinkled or warped as it may make it not possible to scan.
  • When handing out the water, point out the code to the individual, so they know it is there.
  • The stickers should only be placed on the water bottles, not on city infrastructure, or businesses.
  • Bottles that are pre-cooled will need wiped with towel prior to applying QR code sticker,
Teams
  • Report area water distributed in each time so we can track coverage within KC.
  • Dress appropriately for weather, to represent church ministry and sun protection.
  • Be respectful, show kindness and understand cultural diversity.
  • Free Water signs can be checked out, and should be returned for further use.
  • Identification badge lanyards are available; please return when finished using.
  • If asked what organization you are with, Seventh Day Adventist Church.
  • If asked to leave an area (often private /gov't property), please comply.
  • Donations purchased water and QR stickers, please use for distribution only.
