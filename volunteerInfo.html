Suggested Water Sharing Locations
  • Along Street Car Trolley Line
  • Power & Light District
  • Independence Square
  • Swope Park or Loose Park
  • Neighborhood Parks
  • Lawrence Rock Chalk Park / Massachusetts St
  • Country Club Plaza
  • Legends / Sporting KC Area
  • River Market Area
  • Lenexa City Centre Area
  • Sidewalks or trails by churches
  • KS Highways 69 / 52 Rest Area to Route 66
  • Any July 4th Celebration to share!
Teams:
  • Report area water distributed in each time so we can track coverage within KC.
  • Dress appropriately for weather, to represent church ministry and sun protection.
  • Be respectful, show kindness and understand cultural diversity.
  • Free Water signs can be checked out, and should be returned for further use.
  • Identification badge lanyards are available; please return when finished using.
  • If asked what organization you are with, Seventh Day Adventist Church.
  • If asked to leave an area (often private /gov't property), please comply.
  • Bottles that are pre-cooled will need wiped with towel prior to applying QR code sticker,
  • then apply QR code sticker on water bottle label for best visibility.
  • Water can be distributed anytime during week (water pick up only on weekends).
  • Please do not place QR code stickers on business or gov't property. Only water bottles.
  • If possible, bring a cooler chest or bag to pick up water bottle cases (not required).
  • European guests may (often) prefer non cooled water, so be prepared to accommodate.
  • Donations purchased water and QR stickers, please use for distribution only.
